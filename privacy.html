Wander Media uploader privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

Who operates the uploader

Albert Jackson, trading as Wander Media, operates the uploader for Mimi & Friends TV UK, WanderHouse Tribe and Wanderers Rebel Travel. This policy covers the uploader and its publishing records. Contact wandererswindow@gmail.com with privacy questions, complaints or requests.

Google and YouTube data

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. It uses Google authorisation to access the connected YouTube channel and submit videos to YouTube. The channel checks read the channel ID and, in the Travel uploader, the channel title. Uploads can include a title, description, tags, category, visibility, scheduled publication time and made-for-kids setting. The WanderHouse workflow also checks the uploaded video's channel, visibility and processing status.

Google shows the permissions requested when you connect your account. The local uploaders read Google authorisation credentials from files on the publishing computer and use a refresh token to obtain access when needed. YouTube receives the video and publishing details. Google Privacy Policy

Publishing records and other services

Wander Media keeps local publishing records to track uploads, investigate uncertain results and avoid duplicates. Mimi records the file fingerprint, platform, date and returned video ID in a publication database. The video ID can also appear in upload logs and archived filenames. WanderHouse records platform outcomes, returned IDs and links. Travel's local publishing tools record file details, upload outcomes and returned YouTube video IDs in manifests or receipt files.

Travel also has a staging workflow that sends finished videos to Google Drive and registers publishing details in Google Sheets. Its cloud publishing workflow is designed to write YouTube video IDs into a Google Sheets receipt log. Mimi's exported cloud workflow is designed to download approved videos from Drive and record verified YouTube results. Mimi and WanderHouse have separate upload paths that send source videos and publishing details to Facebook and Instagram when those destinations are selected.

Disconnecting and requesting deletion

Open Google Account connections, find the uploader and remove its access. To ask Wander Media to disconnect the uploader or delete information it holds about you, email wandererswindow@gmail.com and identify the affected YouTube channel. Removing access in Google leaves existing local and backup copies in place until they are separately handled. To remove a video from YouTube, use YouTube Studio or another authorised tool that supports deletion on YouTube.
