Who can use the uploader

Albert Jackson, trading as Wander Media, operates the uploader for Mimi & Friends TV UK, WanderHouse Tribe and Wanderers Rebel Travel. Connect a Google account only if you are authorised to manage the intended YouTube channel. Before submitting a video, confirm the channel, content, destinations, visibility, publication time and audience designation. Submit only material you are entitled to publish.

YouTube and Google

The uploader uses YouTube API Services to check the connected channel and send videos and publishing details to YouTube. By using the uploader, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. You are responsible for following those terms and YouTube's applicable content rules. Google and YouTube control access to their services and can require you to authorise the uploader again. If an upload outcome is uncertain, check the channel in YouTube Studio before retrying.

Disconnecting and contacting Wander Media

You can remove the uploader's authorisation in Google Account connections. Read the Uploader Privacy Policy for details about Google account access, publishing records and deletion requests. For help or questions, email wandererswindow@gmail.com.
